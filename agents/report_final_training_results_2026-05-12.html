5. Scientific Takeaways

  • K matters: increasing from K10 to K80 was the major win; AB K80 substantially beats S K10 even with the same full-mix/cut label correction idea.
  • Correct mix/cut supervision matters: mixed images must not be collapsed to a single hard argmax target. The K repeated masked-bit targets should be allocated to the two top labels.
  • Full augmentation is not competitive in the current horizon: Z improved from ep179 to ep199, but remained -8.802 / -7.414 behind AB at matched ep199.
  • Medium augmentation may recover but is still late: AJ improves rapidly after ep39, but at ep79 remains below AB by -3.490 / -2.632.
  • CE sanity remains an open risk: the old 6380 CE sanity reached ep159 65.530; CF current fixes reached only 58.016. Pre-mixup shuffle helps ep19 slightly; approximate GELU alone hurts.

6. Immediate Inference Pivot

Training is paused. The next high-value direction is inference-time scoring on the AB checkpoint: explicitly score candidate class codes under a fixed masked-unmask path and increase inference budget beyond the current logarithmic/top-code style decoder.

Candidate formula for a class code y and a fixed unmask path S_1, ..., S_T:

log p_theta(y | x; path) = sum_t sum_{i in S_t} log p_theta(y_i | x, y_{U_{t-1}}, mask_t)
U_{t-1} = S_1 union ... union S_{t-1}

Because masked diffusion is path-dependent, inference experiments should ablate path choice: one-shot all-masked scoring, MSB-to-LSB, LSB-to-MSB, random fixed paths, confidence-based greedy paths, and multi-path averaging.
