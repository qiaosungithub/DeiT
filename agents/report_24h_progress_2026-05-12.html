6. MixUp/CutMix Label Fix

A major implementation issue in the old runs was mixed images being supervised as a single hard argmax class. Over the last 24h, the useful experiments use the corrected diffusion label path:

  • diffusion_label_mode=soft_top2 takes the top two labels from mixup/cutmix.
  • The K = n_masks_per_image repeated diffusion targets are split between the two labels according to their mixture weights.
  • Example: for K80 and a 50/50 cutmix, 40 mask views train toward label A and 40 train toward label B.
  • This preserves the pure masked-bit objective while making mixup/cutmix semantically correct.

AB is the strongest evidence that corrected mix/cut supervision is useful: it beat prior no-mix K80 references and continued improving late.
