Interpretation

1. Corrected mix/cut labels matter

Using soft_top2 fixes the earlier label-noise problem where mixed images were supervised by a single argmax class. AB's trajectory now clearly exceeds old K10/K80 baselines.

2. CutMix is the stronger component

AF beats AE at ep19 and ep39, and also beats AB at matched ep39. Current hypothesis: cutmix provides useful spatially localized label composition, while mixup-only is weaker for bit diffusion.

3. Full augmentation is not best yet

S proves slow early convergence can be misleading, but even after late catch-up it remains far below AB. Z is still needed for K80 full-aug + mix/cut, but it is very slow.

4. Larger K is not automatically better

Past K160/K320/K640 low-aug no-mix runs did not improve over K80, and AG K160 mixed is not ahead at ep19. Avoid further K increases until AG ep39 is known.

Protocol Guardrails

Do not use warm-start from CE, do not load a pretrained CE backbone, and do not add auxiliary CE loss. The current results are pure diffusion-head training. Same-run checkpoint resume is allowed only for infrastructure failures, as in Z resuming from its own checkpoint_25020.

For collaborator reproduction, the recommended first run is AB exactly. If only one variant can be added, run AF or AH to test the cutmix-heavy hypothesis, but do not replace AB until AF/AH mature checkpoints are available.
